Scale 2.0

Startup incubator based in NYC and Croatia for early-stage Founders  

In Partnership With:

Why apply for Scale 2.0?

Location

We bring Croatian startup founders to Infobip’s campus in Vodnjan, New York City's tech ecosystem, and Infobip's Shift conference in Zadar. Each location is chosen to maximize exposure to innovation, mentorship, and international opportunities.

Dedication

We are committed to support and mentor the further growth of startups and the local startup ecosystem. Our goal is to and expose you to the unique expertise in Vodnjan and NYC’s entrepreneurial ecosystem.

Network

Connect Croatian founders to a global network of industry leaders while fostering a growth mindset while providing access to world-class mentors from Infobip, Goldman Sachs, Columbia StartupLab & others

Objective

You’ll stress-test your startup with expert feedback, refine your product and go-to-market strategy with real-world insights, and pitch on global stages. From the immersive bootcamp in Vodnjan to networking in NYC, and pitching at SHIFT, you'll move faster, think bigger, and build stronger.

Who is the incubator for?

Individuals coming from and residing in Croatia

Have at least 2 founders and one team member

MVP developed

Early stage traction

Founders with solutions in the pre-seed to early stage

Scale 2.0 is a fully funded program covering travel, accommodation, meals, and all program activities across Vodnjan, New York City, and Zadar. We take zero equity — your startup stays 100% yours.

Frequently asked questions

How and when can I apply for Scale 2.0?

You can apply via our website by completing the application form. Follow us on LinkedIn and keep an eye on our site for specific deadlines and details!

What happens after I submit my application?

After submitting your application, our team will review it. If selected, you'll be invited to participate in further stages, which may include a video pitch and an interview with our selection committee.

Who is eligible to apply for Scale 2.0?

Scale 2.0 is open to early-stage founders who are based in Croatia. We welcome founders at the pre-seed to early stage of their startup journey, and the program is industry agnostic, meaning startups from any field are encouraged to apply. To be eligible, your startup must have two founders who are both at least 21 years old. Additionally, applicants must have a developed MVP and validated PMF, alongside an early GTM strategy and demonstrated minimal traction / active users.

What expenses are covered by the incubator?

Scale 2.0 is a fully funded program. This includes travel, accommodation, meals, and program activities, both in Croatia and New York City. No equity is taken from your startup.

Why should I join Scale 2.0?

By joining Scale 2.0, you’ll have the opportunity to immerse yourself in two dynamic entrepreneurial ecosystems—Vodnjan, New York City, and Zadar. You’ll receive ongoing support and mentorship, helping you refine your startup and personal growth. In addition to accessing a global network, you’ll have the chance to connect with top industry experts, successful founders, experienced mentors, investors, and key industry leaders from New York City’s vibrant startup and finance community.

How long is the incubator and what does it include?

Scale 2.0 program consists of three key components: a 5-day in-person program at Infobip’s campus in Vodnjan from May 25-29, a 6-day in-person program in New York City from July 27 - August 1, and a pitch session at Shift in Zadar on September 15. Each part is designed to give you practical experience, mentorship, and the resources needed to scale your startup.

If I’m not accepted, can I apply again next year?

Yes, absolutely! We encourage applicants to refine their ideas and apply again.
