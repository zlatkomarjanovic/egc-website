Entrepreneur in Residence | Founder Residency
‍While Living an Unforgettable Experience.

Build your startup with structured support, mentorship, and access to tools and capital. Work with mentors coming from Google, Linkedin, Microsoft, J.P. Morgan, and read more about our mentors by clicking the button below.

LeapX is opening applications for its Entrepreneur in Residence (EIR) program. This is a founder opportunity, not a regular full-time job and not employment. Participants do not work for LeapX. Instead, they use the program to build their own startup with structured guidance, resources, and investor access.

The program is free for one month and designed for founders who want focused time to move from idea to traction.

About LeapX

LeapX is a one-month intensive startup bootcamp designed for early-stage founders building technology and AI-powered products. The program focuses on execution, validation, and speed, helping founders go from idea or early concept to a working product and a clear next step for growth or fundraising. If you want to replace countless hours of individual work across several solutions and AI tools, and get the guidance on how to build a startup in a super short amount of time, apply today!

This is

  • A founder residency program
  • A structured environment to build your own startup
  • Mentorship, tools, and access to early capital

This is not

  • A regular full-time role
  • Employment or contract work
  • Consulting or agency work

What You Will Get

Founders accepted into the LeapX Entrepreneur in Residence program receive:

Access to 5,000 dollars worth of AI tools to design, build, and test products
Access to up to 20,000 dollars in private funding from LeapX’s investor network
Hands-on mentorship from experienced founders, operators, and investors
Weekly check-ins, structured feedback, and accountability
A curated cohort of ambitious early-stage founders
An optional in-person  residency experience with our mentors in Spain at the Canary Islands

What Founders Actually Do During the Bootcamp

This is a highly execution-focused program. Founders are expected to actively build throughout the four-week online bootcamp. During the program, founders will:

Validate

Refine or validate their startup idea by clearly defining the problem, target customer, and value proposition.

Functional MVP

Build and ship a functional MVP using modern AI and development tools.

Discovery

Conduct customer discovery interviews and validation experiments with real users.

Test

Test assumptions, gather feedback, and iterate on the product based on evidence.

KPIs

Define early traction metrics and run initial growth or distribution experiments.

Real feedback

Receive weekly feedback on product, positioning, and execution from mentors

Roadmap

Prepare a clear roadmap and narrative for continued building or investor conversations

Who Should Apply

This opportunity is a strong fit for:

Founders or aspiring founders, solo or early teams

Individuals with an idea, prototype, or early-stage product

Builders who want structured support and accountability

Founders interested in AI, technology, and scalable products

By the end of the program, founders will have:

A validated startup direction

A comprehensive roadmap that outlines the strategic steps necessary for your startup's growth, ensuring that each phase of development is backed by data-driven insights and market validation.

A working MVP

A working MVP, or Minimum Viable Product, is a crucial step in the startup journey. It represents the simplest version of a product that can be released to the market, allowing founders to test their ideas and gather valuable feedback from early users.

Real customer insights

You will gain real customer insights by engaging directly with your target audience, conducting surveys, and analyzing user feedback. This process will help you understand customer needs, preferences, and pain points, allowing you to refine your product and tailor your marketing strategies effectively.

A clear plan for the next stage of growth or fundraising

a detailed strategy that outlines specific milestones and objectives for scaling your startup, ensuring that each step is supported by thorough market research and financial planning.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply for the LeapX Bootcamp?

You can apply if you are an aspiring startup founder with an early-stage business idea or prototype in the tech or AI space. The call for applications is currently closed.

What happens after I submit my application?

After submitting your application, it will be reviewed by the LeapX team. If selected, you will be invited to participate in the bootcamp, which includes mentorship, workshops, and hands-on sessions designed to accelerate your startup idea.

Who is eligible to apply for this program?

The program is open to aspiring founders who are working on AI-focused or tech-driven startups, either individually or in small teams.

What is the purpose of the LeapX Bootcamp?

LeapX is designed to accelerate early-stage startups by providing mentorship, access to industry experts, practical tools, and networking opportunities. The program aims to equip participants with the skills and connections needed to grow their AI or tech ventures in one month.

Why should I join this program?

Joining LeapX gives you the opportunity to learn from experienced mentors, collaborate with fellow startup founders, refine your business model, and gain exposure to potential investors and partners in the AI and tech ecosystem.

How long is the program and what does it include?

The bootcamp spans several weeks and includes workshops, mentorship sessions, pitch practice, and networking opportunities. Specific schedules and program details can be found on the website.

Will I have opportunities for ongoing collaboration after the program?

Yes. LeapX encourages ongoing collaboration through alumni networks, follow-up mentorship, and opportunities to connect with investors, partners, and fellow founders beyond the bootcamp.
